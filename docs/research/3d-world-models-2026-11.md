# Generated 3D worlds as meshes at the lowest cost per usable world: hosted, open weights on Modal, Quest 3 budgets, a 2 USD test — 2026-11-07

Research date: 2026-11-07. Primary sources only (vendor pricing and API docs, GitHub READMEs and source files, raw LICENSE files, arXiv, Meta developer docs, Modal pricing). Scope, narrowed during the task: **mesh output only** (textured GLB/PLY, or a panorama with depth plus a documented step that makes a mesh). Splat-only routes get one line in § 2.3, video world models in § 2.4. Context: Zumi reads comics in passthrough on Quest 3; a generated tavern shows through a window cut in the real wall, or as a backdrop behind the panels, from a painted panel; a few USD per episode; research project; Argentina **[in-house]**. Routes are ordered by release date, newest first; cost is a column.

Conventions: **[docs]** copied from the cited page. **[est]** my arithmetic from [docs] figures. **[vendor]** a vendor's quality claim. **[secondary]** not the owner's page. **[unverified]** could not be opened or confirmed. Prices in USD.

## Summary

1. **Aug 2026, MoGe-3 (Microsoft, MIT): cheapest documented panorama → textured GLB step, < 0.01 per world on a Modal T4 [est]**. `--glb`: "The color will be saved as a texture"; `infer_panorama` splits an equirectangular image into perspective views and merges a panorama depth map (experimental); 60 ms per view on an A100 ([README](https://github.com/microsoft/MoGe)) **[docs]**. Any panorama (Blockade, Marble's 2560×1280 PNG, a FLUX pano) becomes a mesh bubble.
2. **May 2026, HY-World 2.0 (Tencent): mesh by TSDF fusion, "≥4 GPUs recommended (tested with 8× H20)"** ([worldgen README](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-World-2.0/blob/main/hyworld2/worldgen/README.md)) **[docs]**; cost unverified, far over budget. Territory excludes only EU/UK/South Korea; "Tencent claims no rights in Outputs" ([License.txt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-World-2.0/main/License.txt)) **[docs]**.
3. **Nov 2025, Blockade Labs Skybox AI: GLB export since 2025-11-28, a depth-displaced environment sphere, ~0.20 per skybox on Standard (60 ÷ 300 credits, Model 3 = 1 credit)** ([changelog](https://docs.blockadelabs.com/api/changelog.html), [exports](https://docs.blockadelabs.com/api/skybox-exports.html), [plans](https://skybox.blockadelabs.com/plans)) **[docs, est]**. Not for Model 4; "optimized for visual parallax, not physics".
4. **Nov 2025, World Labs Marble: the only hosted walkable textured mesh, ~4.00 per world via API (1,500 world + 3,500 HQ-mesh credits at 1,250 per USD)**: ~600k triangles with texture maps, 100–200 MB, up to 1 h, artifacts expected ([API pricing](https://docs.worldlabs.ai/api/pricing.md), [mesh](https://docs.worldlabs.ai/marble/export/mesh.md), [specs](https://docs.worldlabs.ai/marble/export/specs.md)) **[docs]**. Free cannot export; World Labs joined AMD 2026-09-28 ([blog](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog)).
5. **Nov 2025, Meta WorldGen: textured 50×50 m scenes with navmesh, "not available to developers"** ([blog](https://www.meta.com/blog/worldgen-3d-world-generation-reality-labs-generative-ai-research/)) **[docs]**. Excluded.
6. **Jul 2025, HunyuanWorld 1.0: the open layered-mesh route, ~0.2–0.4 per world on a Modal A10 (Lite) [est, no official timing]**: per-pixel vertex-coloured PLY layers at up to 3840×1920, no UVs, no decimation ([general_utils.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanWorld-1.0/main/hy3dworld/utils/general_utils.py)) **[docs]**; maintainer: "at least 32GB" full, Lite on a 4090 ([issue 4](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanWorld-1.0/issues/4), [README](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanWorld-1.0)) **[docs]**. Argentina is inside the Territory ([LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanWorld-1.0/main/LICENSE)).
7. **Apr 2025 (updated Apr 2026), WorldGen (ZiYang-xie, Apache-2.0): text/image → 360° bubble as vertex-coloured mesh PLY, "~10GB VRAM" low-VRAM, ~0.05 per world on an L4 [est]**; needs gated FLUX.1-dev ([README](https://github.com/ZiYang-xie/WorldGen)) **[docs]**.
8. **Mar 2023, Text2Room (MIT): room-scale textured mesh PLY from text**, the only walkable open route, 2023 image quality ([README](https://github.com/lukasHoel/text2room)) **[docs]**.
9. **Quest 3: 1.3M–1.8M triangles and 200–1,000 draw calls per frame, 72 FPS minimum** ([Meta](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/unity/unity-perf)); KTX2/Basis textures for WebXR ([Meta](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/web/webxr-perf-bp)) **[docs]**. Marble's 600k triangles and HunyuanWorld's million-vertex layers both need decimation.
10. **Recommendation**: (a) window: panorama → MoGe-3 GLB, ≤ 0.25 per world, hours; (b) backdrop: HunyuanWorld 1.0 Lite on Modal, 1–2 days; Marble at ~4 only for a one-off demo set. Test ≤ 0.40 paid, § 5.

### 2.2 Panorama → mesh, the documented step

`moge infer_panorama -i pano.png --o out --glb` (confirm flags in `--help`; the README documents `--glb/--ply` on `moge infer` and says infer_panorama "splits a 360° image into perspective views… combines the results into a panorama depth map and point map") ([MoGe README](https://github.com/microsoft/MoGe)) **[docs]**. Result: one textured sheet, parallax for tens of centimetres, holes if the user walks. HunyuanWorld's scene stage adds layers and a sky; Blockade's GLB is the hosted version with a `depth_scale` slider. Panorama sources: Blockade Model 3 (0.20), Marble's PNG (Standard), or `demo_panogen.py` alone (478 MB LoRA over Flux).

## 3. Rendering the mesh in our engines

**Quest 3 budgets** ([Meta Unity perf](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/unity/unity-perf)) **[docs]**: "a minimum of 72 FPS"; triangles "1.3m-1.8m" per frame; draw calls 200–300 busy, 400–600 medium, 700–1,000 light; triangles spanning several tiles cost more, so one huge sphere costs more than its count suggests. Zumi's frame already carries passthrough, panels and bubbles **[in-house]**: budget ≤ 400k triangles and ≤ 20 draw calls for a backdrop, ≤ 150k for a window **[est]**. **Textures**: "KTX 2.0/Basis Universal is the recommended approach… the in-memory size… will be smaller" ([Meta WebXR](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/web/webxr-perf-bp)) **[docs]**; a 4096×2048 RGBA8 panorama is 32 MB plus mips, ETC2/ASTC cuts it 4–8× **[est]**.

**three.js**: `GLTFLoader` + `DRACOLoader` + `KTX2Loader`, the chain in Meta's Flap Frenzy sample ([sample](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/web/web-sample-flap-frenzy)) **[docs]**. **Unity**: glTFast 6.20 imports GLB at runtime with "KTX/Basis Universal, Draco, and meshoptimizer", all pipelines ([manual](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.cloud.gltfast@6.20/manual/index.html)) **[docs]**. **Conditioning**: gltfpack (MIT, npm) ([meshoptimizer](https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer)) **[docs]** for Marble's 600k-triangle, 100–200 MB GLB and HunyuanWorld's per-pixel PLY; HunyuanWorld's vertex colours need a bake to a UV texture (xatlas, as the paper's offline pipeline) or `KHR_materials_unlit`.

**Window into a world**: draw the mesh only where a quad on the real wall passes a stencil test (Unity `Stencil`, [docs](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/current/manual/materials-and-shaders/shaders/reference/sl-reference/sl-commands/sl-stencil); three.js `webgl_portal`, [example](https://threejs.org/examples/webgl_portal.html)) **[docs]**. The viewer sees one hemisphere from a near-fixed point, so a depth sphere (Blockade or MoGe GLB) suffices and holes never show. **Full backdrop**: ~0.5 m of head motion reveals occlusions, so layers (HunyuanWorld) or a walkable mesh (Marble, Text2Room) are needed; draw the sky last to avoid overdraw ([Meta WebXR](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/web/webxr-perf-bp)) **[docs]**.

## 4. Stylised output from a painted panel

No route has a style switch; style enters through the input. **Blockade**: `skybox_style_id` required, from `GET /skybox/styles`; `control_image` "Keeps the structure of the image but not its colors"; `init_image` keeps colours ([API](https://docs.blockadelabs.com/api/skybox.html)) **[docs]**. **Marble**: "decoupling structure from style", cartoon and anime claimed ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/?p=720746)) **[vendor, secondary]**; no independent input-vs-output test found. **HunyuanWorld / WorldGen**: the pano stage is a Flux model conditioned on the image, so palette and line carry **[est]**; the paper claims "diverse artistic styles" without examples ([arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.21809v2)) **[vendor]**. **The risk is depth, not colour**: every mesh route lifts the pano with a monocular depth model, and flat comic colour is where image-to-3D "struggle[s]" per Art3D (`3d-generative-cheapest-2026-11.md`, Summary 6) **[cross-ref]**; shade the reference first, or accept flatter relief and raise `depth_scale`.

## 5. Recommendation and test plan

Ranked by cost per usable world, then hours **[est]**.

**(a) Tavern through one wall window.** 1. Panorama → **MoGe-3 GLB** on a Modal T4: ≤ 0.25 with a Blockade Model 3 skybox (0.20), ~0.03 with a FLUX pano via the BFL API the pipeline already calls (0.014 per MP); hours to wire; MIT step. 2. **Blockade GLB export** directly (0.20, Standard 60/month): fewer parts, a subscription. 3. **HunyuanWorld 1.0 Lite** (0.2–0.4): overkill for a window.

**(b) Full backdrop set.** 1. **HunyuanWorld 1.0 Lite on a Modal A10** (0.2–0.4 per world; 1–2 days: Flux base, LoRAs, MoGe and segmentation in the `zumi-models` Volume, then decimation and a vertex-colour bake): parallax layers and sky, licence valid in Argentina. 2. **Marble API** (4.00 per world, hours): the only walkable textured mesh, over the per-episode budget, right for one demo set; the 5.00 minimum buys one. 3. **Text2Room** only if walkable open output is mandatory.

**Test, ≤ 0.40 paid.** Reference: one shaded tavern panel.

1. **Blockade, 0.00**: Free plan, web UI, a painterly Model 3 style, `init_image` = the panel; download the 8K JPG and depth map; 3D Creator "Download GLB" at Medium, depth 5. If GLB is paywalled on Free, keep the JPG. Free assets are non-commercial (ToS §9).
2. **MoGe-3 on a Modal T4, ~0.01 (Starter credit)**: `moge infer_panorama -i skybox.jpg --o out --glb`; also `moge infer -i panel.png --version v3 --pretrained … --glb` for a perspective-only bubble.
3. **HunyuanWorld 1.0 Lite on a Modal A10, ≤ 0.37**: 20 min timeout via `run_episodes.py`'s budget → timeout; the two commands in § 2.1.
4. **Condition**: `gltfpack -i in.glb -o out.glb -si 0.2 -tc`; for PLY layers, Blender Decimate to 100k → bake vertex colour to a 2048² texture → GLB.

**Acceptance**, each a recorded number: loads in a three.js `GLTFLoader` + `KTX2Loader` page with no console error; ≤ 300k triangles and ≤ 20 draw calls (`renderer.info`); file ≤ 25 MB; frame time ≤ 8 ms at 2× 1680×1760 on the laptop GPU (300-frame median), headroom under the 13.9 ms Quest frame; no visible hole on a 0.5 m lateral head move (backdrop case); licence line in the asset's JSON (Blockade Free non-commercial; MoGe MIT; HunyuanWorld Community, Argentina in Territory, NOTICE kept).
