# Text-to-speech for Zumi character voices — state of the market, 2026-10-15

Research date: 2026-10-15. Primary sources only (model cards, licence files, provider docs and pricing pages, Artificial Analysis leaderboard pages). Where a page could not be read (client-rendered, gated, 404) that is flagged and no number is given. Requirements: (1) distinct, consistent voice per character from a text description or a short clip; (2) expressive delivery (emotion, shout, whisper); (3) commercial licence; (4) API or self-hostable on Modal.

## Executive summary

1. Meta has **no text-to-speech model** available in any form on 2026-10-15. Meta Model API lists Muse Wonder/Spark/Image/Voice Transcribe/SAM 3.1/Glimmer; the only audio model, Muse Voice Transcribe, "does not synthesize speech". Audiobox/Voicebox never shipped weights; Spirit LM and SeamlessExpressive are gated, noncommercial; MMS-TTS is open but CC-BY-NC 4.0 and has no cloning. A "Muse Realtime Voice" with describable voices was shown at Connect 2026-09-23 but only as a consumer feature, no API, no weights.
2. Closed leader for the use case: **ElevenLabs Eleven v4** (Artificial Analysis Speech Arena #1–2, Elo 1326–1328), free-form audio tags (`[whispers]`, `[shouts]`, combinable emotions), Voice Design API from a text prompt (`POST /v1/text-to-voice/design`, saved as reusable `voice_id`), $0.08/1K chars (v4) or $0.04 (v4 Turbo); commercial use on any paid plan.
3. Cheapest strong closed alternative: **Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS** (Elo 1273): voice design from text (`POST /v1beta/voices`, persistent `voice_` ID), replication with consent audio, `speech_metadata.style` plus inline `<laugh>`/`<sigh>`, 130+ languages, $9/M audio tokens (= $0.81/h of audio) through 2026-12-31, doubling 2027-01-01.
4. Hume Octave is **sunsetting 2026-11-13** — exclude. OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-tts has 13 fixed voices and gated custom voices (sales-approved, consent recording), no text voice design. Cartesia Sonic 3.6 clones from 10 s but documents no text voice design. MiniMax Speech 2.8 has both ($3/voice design, $1.50/clone, $60–100/M chars).
5. Open-weight with a clean commercial licence **and** text-description voice design: **Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign** (Apache 2.0, 10 languages, `instruct` string describes the voice, Jan 2026) and **Maya1** (Apache 2.0, 3.3B, `<description="...">` wrapper, 20 emotion tags incl. `<whisper>`, `<scream>`; 16 GB VRAM). Cloning + MIT: Chatterbox (Resemble, 500M, 23 languages, `[laugh]` tags on Turbo, exaggeration knob). Not usable commercially: Fish S2 Pro (research licence), Fish Drama 3 (Community Licence, <$500K revenue and no hosted service), Higgs TTS 3, Breeze TTS 2, Voxtral TTS (CC-BY-NC).
6. Leaderboards (AA, read 2026-10-15): all top 9 are closed; best open weight is Fish Audio Drama 3 Preview (Elo 1242, #10). Open-weights board: Drama 3 1242, Breeze TTS 2 1220, S2 Pro 1116, Step-Audio-EditX 1096, Voxtral TTS 1088, Kokoro 1061. Hugging Face Open TTS Leaderboard (2026-09-30) ranks by WER/speed/speaker-similarity, not preference.
7. Cost (§F, assumption 900 chars/min = 54K chars per audio-hour): Eleven v4 $4.32/h, v4 Turbo $2.16/h; Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS **$0.81/h** (Elo 1273, voice design + consent cloning + style tags); Inworld Realtime TTS-2 $0.81–1.35/h (Elo 1259, voice design + cloning, Aug 2026); Qwen-Audio-3.1-TTS-Plus $1.04/h (Elo 1297); Speechify Simba 3.2 $0.36/h (Elo 1243, no voice design); Azure DragonHD Omni ≈$1.19/h (50+ style tags incl. `whispering`/`shouting`, no instant design); Polly Generative $1.62/h (Elo 1064, no design). Self-host on Modal L40S is ≤$1.95 per audio-hour even at real-time factor 1.0 (no RTF published for Qwen3-TTS, Chatterbox V3 or Maya1).
8. Recommendation: Meta option — none (state it explicitly). **Best cheap: Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS** — the only sub-$1/h API with text voice design, persistent voice IDs, a style field and inline vocal events, Elo within 55 of ElevenLabs. **Best mid: Inworld Realtime TTS-2** (voice design + cloning + natural-language steering, Elo 1259) or ElevenLabs **v4 Turbo** ($2.16/h, Elo 1328) if the A/B shows the ElevenLabs designed voice holds character better. ElevenLabs v4 full ($4.32/h) is 5.3× Gemini for +53 Elo; not worth it unless the A/B proves it. Open fallback on Modal: Qwen3-TTS 1.7B VoiceDesign + Base, Chatterbox Multilingual V3 second.
9. Uncertainties: no leaderboard measures *voice-design consistency across many lines*, which is Zumi's core need; Gemini 3.8 TTS prices double on 2027-01-01 ($1.62/h, still cheapest with design); Inworld TTS-2 and Qwen-Audio-3.1 are Aug–Sept 2026 releases with one month of public track record; ElevenLabs plan credit maths conflict between its two pricing pages; Azure/Resemble/Speechify/Hume pricing pages are client-rendered — their $/M figures come from the Artificial Analysis price column, not the vendor page; self-host $/h is an upper bound, not a measurement.

### Self-hosting on Modal (upper bounds, not measurements)

None of the three candidates publishes a real-time factor: Qwen3-TTS gives only "latency as low as 97 ms" to first packet; Chatterbox gives "3x faster than realtime on 8 CPU cores" for Nano only; Maya1 says "streaming latency below 100 ms, depending on hardware". So the honest figure is a bound: with Modal L40S at $0.000542/s ($1.95/h) and A10 at $0.000306/s ($1.10/h), **one audio-hour costs ≤ $1.95 (L40S) or ≤ $1.10 (A10) even at RTF 1.0**, and proportionally less at RTF 0.2–0.5, which a 0.5–2B autoregressive model at batch 1 on an L40S typically reaches (reasoning, unmeasured). Maya1 needs ≥16 GB VRAM, so A10 (24 GB) is the floor. Caveats: Modal bills from container start, so a cold start (image pull + 2–7 GB weight load, tens of seconds) is paid on every scale-from-zero; comic rendering is bursty, so either keep one warm container (`min_containers=1`, ≈$1,400/month on L40S) or accept 20–60 s first-request latency; Modal's $30/month Starter credit covers ~15 L40S-hours. Sources: https://modal.com/pricing ; https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS ; https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox ; https://huggingface.co/maya-research/maya1

### Quality per dollar for Zumi

Zumi needs (a) a designed voice that persists across every line a character speaks, (b) tags for shout/whisper/emotion, (c) commercial terms, (d) API. Providers that document all four: ElevenLabs v4/Turbo, Gemini 3.8 Flash (and Flash-Lite), Inworld Realtime TTS-2 / TTS-2 Flash, MiniMax 2.8. Azure HD Omni has the richest style-tag list but no instant voice design; Speechify and Qwen-Audio-3.1 have the Elo but no documented voice design; Cartesia and Deepgram lack it outright.

| Rank | Pick | $/audio-h | Elo | Why | Proven? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Best cheap** | Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS | $0.81 (→ $1.62 in 2027) | 1273 | All four requirements documented; persistent `voice_` IDs; 130+ languages; 5.3× cheaper than Eleven v4, 2.7× cheaper than Turbo, 55 Elo behind | GA 2026-09-22; the 3.1 TTS preview preceded it since early 2026 |
| **Best mid** | Inworld Realtime TTS-2 (on-demand; Developer plan if >20 h/month) | $1.35 ($0.81) | 1259 | Voice design + cloning on the $0 commercial plan; natural-language emotion steering; 200+ languages | Aug 2026; docs gated behind login; least proven of the three |
| **Premium** | ElevenLabs v4 Turbo | $2.16 | 1328 | Highest Elo; most mature Voice Design API (three previews, `guidance_scale`); largest voice library; combinable free-form tags | Mature product; v4 itself Sept 2026 |
| Avoid paying for | ElevenLabs v4 (non-Turbo) | $4.32 | 1326 | Same Elo as Turbo within the ±12–16 interval, 2× the price; the 10,000-char request limit is the only documented difference | — |

Is ElevenLabs worth the premium? Not on the leaderboards: v4 Turbo vs Gemini 3.8 Flash is +55 Elo for 2.7× the price. It is justified only if a Zumi-specific A/B (5 character descriptions × 20 lines, blind rating of "same character?" and "did it shout/whisper?") shows ElevenLabs' designed voices hold identity better across lines — which no public board measures. Run that A/B on Gemini 3.8 Flash vs Eleven v4 Turbo vs Inworld TTS-2 before committing; at these rates the whole test costs under $5.
