# Re-skinning the real room in mixed reality: research lineage, what ships on Quest, techniques and limits — 2026-11-07

Research date: 2026-11-07. Primary sources only (papers and their PDFs, Meta/Apple/Google/PICO developer docs, official sample repos and Meta engineering blogs); press is marked **[secondary]**. Context: Zumi reads comics on Quest 3 in passthrough; `docs/design/room-dressing-2026-11.md` v7 dresses the room with one lit prop on a relit proxy and one passthrough LUT per setting. The question here is the next family up: turning the whole room into the story's place.

Conventions: **[docs]** = copied from the cited page or paper. **[est]** = my arithmetic or inference. **[secondary]** = not the owner's page. **[unverified]** = full text not reachable today. "Re-skin" = change what the room's surfaces look like while keeping their layout; "replace" = full VR.

## Summary (ranked for our case)

1. **The one measured answer to "does partial re-skinning beat full VR?" is yes, at a safety cost.** Roomify (CHI 2026, N=18) kept the room's layout and swapped surfaces and furniture for generated ones: presence 5.94 vs 4.72 fully-virtual vs 3.65 passthrough (IPQ, p<0.01, p<0.001), but spatial awareness 5.10 vs 6.20 in passthrough (p<0.01) and self-reported incidents 0.56 vs 0.22 ([Roomify](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04917)) **[docs]**. It is offline: 19:46 min per room on cloud GPUs and paid image/3D APIs **[docs]**.
2. **Meta tells developers not to do it with the scene mesh**: "Do not use scene mesh for reskinning or stylizing the environment", it "can lead to improper occlusion and difficulty navigating", "drift and misalignment can occur", and "content that contains depth affordance (e.g., portals) may also entice users to peer through or walk into the wall" ([MR health: Scene](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/design/mr-health-scene/)) **[docs]**. MRUK ships the tools anyway: a `Virtual Home` sample that "reskin[s] the real world with a customizable prefab-based interior layout using semantic anchors" ([MRUK samples](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-mr-utility-kit-samples)) **[docs]**.
3. **Live restyling of the passthrough image is not available to apps.** Passthrough "is rendered by a dedicated service into a separate layer" and "apps cannot access images or videos of a user's physical environment" ([Passthrough](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-passthrough/)) **[docs]**; style controls are LUTs, brightness/contrast/saturation and edges ([colour mapping](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-customize-passthrough-color-mapping/)) **[docs]**. The Passthrough Camera API copies the frames (60 Hz, 20–40 ms latency) into a texture that "covers a smaller rectangle than the user's view" ([PCA](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-pca-overview/)) **[docs]**: a neural restyle can drive textures or lighting, not replace the view.
4. **Every shipped Quest title that "transforms the room" keeps passthrough and adds openings or props**: First Encounters punches Voronoi holes in a passthrough-shaded scene mesh and keeps everything below 0.75 m intact ([Meta engineering](https://developers.meta.com/vr/blog/scene-mesh-destruction-first-encounters-meta-quest-developers-mixed-reality/)) **[docs]**; Ocean Rift and Starship Home hang windows on walls ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/how-creature-approached-mixed-reality-design-challenges-with-starship-home/)) **[secondary]**. None retextures the whole room.
5. **The lineage's constant finding (2015–2019)**: substitutes are tolerated until shape or function breaks (Simeone 2015); people ignore virtual boundaries (Oasis 2016); full replacement raises presence but lowers felt safety (DreamWalker 2019) **[docs]**. Fade transitions keep continuity best (Mdn 4.67 vs 3.17, N=16) ([Pointecker 2022](https://www.imis.uni-luebeck.de/sites/default/files/2023-03/532500a827.pdf)) **[docs]**.
6. **Recommendation**: stay in the hybrid cell (§ 4e). A weekend buys one tavern window on a WALL_FACE anchor behind a depth-occluded alpha hole; a month buys semantic retexture of walls and floor with the key wall and furniture left as passthrough; a quarter buys a Roomify-style offline pipeline, a different product. Stop at the month.

**Generative follow-ups (2023–2026)**, all offline restyles of a captured scene:

- *Text2Room* (ICCV 2023): text → textured room mesh, no input room ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11989)); 70 min ([FastScene](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.05768)) to 13.3 h ([DreamScene](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.03575)) per scene **[docs]**. *RoomDreamer* (Apple 2023): phone-scanned mesh + prompt → new geometry and texture ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11337)). *SceneTex* (2023): textures for an untextured room mesh by depth-to-image diffusion ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17261)). *Instruct-NeRF2NeRF* (ICCV 2023) re-optimises a NeRF per edit ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12789)). *StyleGaussian* (SIGGRAPH Asia 2024): zero-shot style on a reconstructed Gaussian scene "at 10 frames per second" on a desktop GPU ([page](https://kunhao-liu.github.io/StyleGaussian/)) **[docs]**.
- *SpaceBlender* (2024): users' rooms blended into one VR meeting space; N=20 valued familiarity but "some found the generated environments complex enough to distract from the task" ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13926)) **[docs]**. *Editing Reality* (DIS 2026): speech-driven add / erase / retexture of scanned objects in MR, generation on a local GPU server, pilot N=14 ([project](https://www.colorado.edu/atlas/editing-reality-situ-co-creation-generative-ai-mixed-reality), [poster](https://www.colorado.edu/atlas/sites/default/files/attached-files/weng-editing_reality.pdf)) **[docs]**. *ImaginateAR* (UIST 2025): pre-scanned outdoor scene → assets in ~30 s, N=20 ([PDF](https://jessvb.github.io/assets/pdf/UIST_2025_ImaginateAR.pdf)) **[docs]**. *Hybrelighter* (2025): on-device MR relighting "as high as 100 fps" that corrects reconstruction errors; relights, does not restyle ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14930)) **[docs]**.
- *Roomify* (CHI 2026) is the room-scale restyle with numbers. Input: a 30–60 s monocular Quest 3 video plus a style prompt; SLAM3R → SpatialLM labelled boxes; gpt-image-1 views, Tripo v2.5 furniture, tileable wall/floor textures, a Skybox AI panorama for the ceiling; assets stream to Unity over Link. 19:46 min and 16.93 manual edits per room; a rectangular table turned round and hurt distance judgement ([Roomify](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04917)) **[docs]**.
- Meta's *Hyperscape Capture* (Connect 2025): room → Gaussian splats in minutes of scanning and hours of processing, cloud-rendered ([RadianceFields](https://radiancefields.com/meta-announces-meta-horizon-engine-and-capture)) **[secondary]**; capture only, no API.

## 2. Shipped on Meta Quest

**Scene mesh and anchors.** The scene mesh is "a low-fidelity, high-coverage artifact which describes the boundary between free and occupied space", "generated automatically during the space setup experience" ([scene mesh](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unreal/unreal-scene-mesh/)) **[docs]**, static until Space Setup is re-run ([UploadVR](https://uploadvr.com/quest-multi-room-mixed-reality-support)) **[secondary]**; anchors carry labels with a 2D boundary or 3D box ([Meta Reality](https://www.meta.com/blog/mixed-reality-definition-passthrough-scene-understanding-spatial-anchors/)) **[docs]**.

**EffectMesh** (MRUK) is the re-skinning primitive: `MeshMaterial` on every anchor passing a `Labels` filter, "if you'd like a multi-material room, you can use another EffectMesh object with a different Mesh Material"; wall UVs `METRIC`, `METRIC_SEAMLESS`, `MAINTAIN_ASPECT_RATIO(_SEAMLESS)`, `STRETCH`, `STRETCH_SECTION`; `CutHoles` "for door frames and/or window frames"; `Colliders`; `BorderSize` ([API](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/reference/mruk/v85/class_meta_x_r_m_r_utility_kit_effect_mesh/)) **[docs]**; no triplanar mode. The *Passthrough Relighting* sample's `HighlightsAndShadows` shader (Zumi's relit proxy) adds point-light highlights and "marks shadow areas adjusting transparency", "blended with the Passthrough layer" in the compositor ([relighting](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-passthrough-relighting/)) **[docs]**.

**Passthrough styling.** A LUT "maps each RGB color to an arbitrary new RGB(A) color"; `SetColorLut(lut, weight)` "can be called in every frame"; colour controls "are mutually exclusive" ([colour mapping](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-customize-passthrough-color-mapping/)) **[docs]**. Design guidance lists "blending the gradient mask, grayscale re-lighting, tinting the passthrough images, adjusting the camera's background opacity, or stylizing passthrough edges" and warns that abrupt transitions are "overwhelming and uncomfortable" ([passthrough design](https://developers.meta.com/vr/design/mr-design-passthrough/)) **[docs]**.

**Passthrough Camera API** (OS v74+, Quest 3/3S): 1280×960, 1280×1280 (v83), 1728×1728 (v209); "Data rate: 60Hz"; "image capture latency of 20-40ms"; "GPU overhead of roughly 1-2% per streamed camera"; stated uses include "using lighting or the camera image to modify textures" ([PCA](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-pca-overview/)) **[docs]**; samples `ShaderSample` and `MultiObjectDetection` (Unity Inference Engine) ([repo](https://github.com/oculus-samples/Unity-PassthroughCameraApiSamples)) **[docs]**. **Depth API**: dynamic occlusion the Scene Model cannot do; minimum ~0.2 m ([Depth API](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-depthapi-overview/)) **[docs]**. Passthrough is composited "after the entire app has rendered" ([Meta GDC](https://developers.meta.com/vr/blog/optimizing-for-success-meta-quest-3-gdc/)) **[docs]**; measured latency 35–40 ms vs ~11 ms on Vision Pro (Optofidelity, [via Mixed](https://mixed-news.com/en/quest-3-vision-pro-passthrough-latency-measurement/)) **[secondary]**.

**Meta samples.** *First Encounters*: "by applying a Passthrough shader to the material of the Scene Mesh" the room looks normal until holes open; `DestructibleMesh` uses "Voronoi partitioning to sort the Scene Mesh triangles into submesh chunks"; triangles inside a 0.3 m floor inset and below a 0.75 m plane are "reserved as indestructible" ([Meta engineering, 2023-12-01](https://developers.meta.com/vr/blog/scene-mesh-destruction-first-encounters-meta-quest-developers-mixed-reality/)) **[docs]**. *The World Beyond*: room anchors, depth occlusion, a `PassthroughStylist`, a "wall manipulator"; passthrough "as an underlay" revealed "by modifying the application's alpha level" ([doc](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-sample-the-world-beyond), [PassthroughImplementation.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oculus-samples/Unity-TheWorldBeyond/main/Documentation/PassthroughImplementation.md)) **[docs]**. *Phanto* (scene-mesh navigation, [repo](https://github.com/oculus-samples/Unity-Phanto)) and *Discover* (colocation, [repo](https://github.com/oculus-samples/Unity-Discover)) do not restyle **[docs]**.

**Third-party titles.** *Ocean Rift* MR (2023-12): user-drawn rectangles become windows, an optional skylight replaces the ceiling, passthrough tinted blue, walls from room setup ([UploadVR](https://uploadvr.com/ocean-rift-mixed-reality-turns-your-room-into-aquarium)) **[secondary]**. *Starship Home* (2024-09): windows and panels on walls from the scene mesh; Creature: the system gives "a rough 3D mesh. That's all, nothing else"; a floor beacon anchors the layout; "the paintings that are hanging on your wall now hang on the wall of the starship" ([interview](https://www.uploadvr.com/how-creature-approached-mixed-reality-design-challenges-with-starship-home/)) **[secondary]**. *Laser Dance*: "the main one is the mesh API" ([UploadVR](https://uploadvr.com/laser-dance-mixed-reality-impressions)); one spatial anchor for the scene, "scene drift" cost dev time ([UploadVR](https://uploadvr.com/quest-multi-room-mixed-reality-support)) **[secondary]**. *Spatial Ops* (2024-11): "portals will open up in your environment", couches as cover, "mapping, occlusion, spatial anchors" ([Meta blog](https://www.meta.com/blog/spatial-ops-launch/)) **[docs]**. *Wall Town Wonders* builds on real walls ([press](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/wall-town-wonders-is-out-now-on-meta-quest-3-3s-help-the-tiny-people-who-live-in-your-walls-)); *Demeo* MR puts a fixed-size board on the real table ([Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/demeo-mixed-reality-update-quest-pro/)) **[secondary]**. Pattern: portals and props on anchors; passthrough stays.

## 3. Apple Vision Pro and others

visionOS has three immersion styles: `mixed` ("intermixed with... passthrough video"), `progressive` ("partially replaces passthrough video", with initial/min/max amounts and an aspect ratio, driven by the Digital Crown), `full` ("completely replaces passthrough video") ([ImmersionStyle](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/immersionstyle), [Progressive](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/progressiveimmersionstyle)) **[docs]**. In full immersion "visionOS defines a system boundary that extends approximately 1.5 meters from the initial position of the person's head. If their head moves outside of that zone, the system automatically stops the immersive experience and turns on the external video again" ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/creating-fully-immersive-experiences)) **[docs]**: Environments are a seated replacement with a hard leash, not a re-skin. Room-aware work goes through ARKit `MeshAnchor` (per-face `MeshClassification`) and `OcclusionMaterial` ([MeshAnchor](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/arkit/meshanchor), [sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/obscuring-virtual-items-in-a-scene-behind-real-world-items)) **[docs]**; occluders also hide system windows ([forum](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/765807)) **[docs]**. No textured-mesh or passthrough-styling API.

Android XR's `SpatialEnvironment` holds "either / both a skybox and a glTF for geometry" toggled against passthrough by an opacity preference ([reference](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/xr/scenecore/SpatialEnvironment)) **[docs]**: replacement only. PICO's Unity SDK has Spatial Mesh since 3.0 ("dynamically scanning the physical environment in real time"), on the PICO 4 series in 3.4.0 (2026-02) ([3.0](https://github.com/Pico-Developer/PICO-Unity-Integration-SDK/releases/tag/release_3.0.0), [3.4.0](https://github.com/Pico-Developer/PICO-Unity-Integration-SDK/releases/tag/release_3.4.0)) **[docs]**; nothing on restyling.

## 4. Techniques, compared

Frame budgets: 13.9 ms at 72 Hz, 11.1 ms at 90 Hz, 8.3 ms at 120 Hz ([Meta perf](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/po-perf-opt-mobile/)) **[docs]**.

| Technique | Headset today | GPU | What breaks | Latency vs passthrough |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) **Retexture the scene mesh by label** (EffectMesh, METRIC_SEAMLESS, CutHoles) | yes; `Virtual Home` is the sample | a few opaque draws **[est]** | mesh is "low-fidelity"; walls off the real plane show double edges and swallow pictures and shelves; moving things need Depth API; Meta says no | none: app content, timewarped with the world |
| (b) **Generated textures/panorama per room** (Roomify, SceneTex, RoomDreamer) | offline, loaded at runtime | as (a) once baked | 20 min and paid APIs per room; hallucinated geometry; redo after Space Setup | none at runtime |
| (c) **Portals/windows on walls** (Ocean Rift, Starship Home, Spatial Ops, World Beyond) | yes: alpha hole in the passthrough underlay + depth-occluded world behind | one alpha pass + the world behind **[est]** | "may entice users to... walk into the wall" (Meta); must sit on a real WALL_FACE, off the key wall | none |
| (d) **Live passthrough restyle** (LUT/edges; neural via PCA) | LUT/edges in the compositor; neural only as app content | LUT ~free; PCA 1–2% per camera + inference; phone style nets run >25 fps ([Kunster](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.03415)) **[docs]** | camera texture is smaller than the view, 20–40 ms old, and lacks the compositor's stereo reconstruction ([Passthrough+](http://szeliski.org/papers/Chaurasia_Passthrough_CGIT20.pdf)); a full-view replacement would swim **[est]** | adds camera + inference on top of the 35–40 ms the compositor hides |
| (e) **Hybrid: real surfaces kept, decor replaced** (relit proxy + grade + one window + props) | yes, all MRUK/OVR | (a)+(c) on one or two anchors | LUT-graded passthrough vs lit props mismatch | none |

Two facts settle the table. Passthrough is composited after the app, so every re-skin is geometry over an image the app can hide (alpha) or brighten (HighlightsAndShadows) but never read. And mesh and anchors are a Space Setup snapshot: Meta concedes drift and "difficulty navigating" when they stand in for the room; shipped titles compensate with margins (First Encounters' 0.75 m) or by keeping passthrough everywhere except openings.

## 5. Design findings

- **Partial re-skinning beats full VR on presence and keeps most awareness.** Roomify: presence +63% over passthrough, +26% over a furniture-less full-VR skybox; spatial awareness 5.10 vs 4.41 full-VR (p<0.05), below passthrough's 6.20; incidents 0.56 / 0.22 / 0.94; 14/18 preferred it **[docs]**. Caveat: the full-VR baseline had no furniture and an active boundary, which "may underestimate its immersion" **[docs]**.
- **What must stay.** Roomify's participants wanted "the overall layout and room structure, the approximate volume, footprint, and major contact surfaces of each object, the high-level function of each object" kept, and asked to lock key items; style, materials and the ceiling were free; "for movement-heavy use, safety-critical objects should keep strict geometric consistency" **[docs]**. Simeone 2015 says the same from the other side: discrepancy is fine until it reaches shape or function **[docs, abstract]**.
- **Boundaries get ignored.** Oasis had to steer users away from obstacles; DreamWalker's live detection raised presence yet users felt safer seeing the depth view **[docs]**; Meta: "the more physical objects left within the boundary that are not represented by virtual content, the greater the risk" ([health](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/design/mr-health-scene/)) **[docs]**.
- **Transitions.** "Users preferred Fade in a workplace setting"; Portal was "visually exciting" but no better on continuity (Mdn 3.50 vs 4.67) ([Pointecker 2022](https://www.imis.uni-luebeck.de/sites/default/files/2023-03/532500a827.pdf)) **[docs]**; Apple wants a window first and a user-started transition because "sudden transitions might be disorienting" **[docs]**.
- **Generated rooms can distract from the task** (SpaceBlender, N=20) **[docs]**: the finding that bears most on a reader whose panels must hold attention.

**Verdict.** Re-skinning fits Zumi only up to level 2, and level 1 is worth doing first. A kept layout with changed surfaces raises presence over passthrough without full VR's safety collapse, the right direction for a story-driven reader; but the same studies say generated rooms pull attention (SpaceBlender), the mesh cannot be trusted at object scale (Meta, Roomify), and every shipped Quest title stops at windows and props. The panels are what the reader must look at; the room is where the eyes rest. A tavern window the candle lights, and later tavern walls fading in behind passthrough furniture, give "the tavern takes over" without competing with the key wall. A per-room generative pipeline is a different product, for after the reader ships.
