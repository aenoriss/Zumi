# 3D sets and props for Zumi: agentic Blender, asset libraries, generative 3D, procedural and NPR routes, Blender on Modal — 2026-11-07

Research date: 2026-11-07. Primary sources only (GitHub repos and LICENSE files, arXiv papers, model cards, vendor API and pricing pages, Blender manual and release notes, Modal docs, live API calls), read by five parallel agents; secondary sources are named as such. Context: Zumi's step 11 "3D scene frames" (`docs/architecture/decisions.md` §4) says an agent drives headless Blender through MCP in a Modal container with "primitives, curated low-poly library, materials, lights"; the hand-written-primitives part produced toy-like results in Nov 2026 **[in-house]**. Settings (step 10, `pipeline/segment/settings.py`) already give each frame a place; props hang on them (`CONTEXT.md`, "Setting"). The Unity reader loads `scene.glb` with glTFast (`apps/xr/Assets/Zumi/Reader/ZumiPanelScene.cs`).

Conventions: **[docs]** = copied from the cited page. **[live]** = an API call made today. **[est]** = my arithmetic from [docs] figures, or an inference. **[in-house]** = observed in our pipeline. **[secondary]** = not the owner's page. **[unverified]** = could not be checked. A "setting" is one place (the taproom); a "box" is one panel.

## Summary (ranked for our case)

1. **Why primitives failed, with numbers**: the 2026 benchmarks say LLM-written bpy geometry is a shape-quality ceiling, not a tooling gap. On 3DCodeBench (212 categories, Blender 5.0 scripts, 12 VLMs) agentic retries lift executability from ~0.70 to ~0.97 but "do not improve shape quality"; renders "often have disconnected or floating parts" ([3DCodeBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.01057v1)) **[docs]**. SceneCraft only beat BlenderGPT on aesthetics (74.5% human preference) by *retrieving* assets from Turbosquid; without its relation graph its scores collapse from 69.8/88.9 to 19.4/3.2 ([SceneCraft](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.01248)) **[docs]**. More MCP tools or skill packs (arjun988/blender-skills has 94 skills; blenderwright 276 tools) do not change this **[est]**.
2. **Rank 1 route: retrieve textured assets and let the LLM emit relational constraints, not coordinates (Holodeck pattern).** Holodeck's ablation: GPT-4 absolute coordinates score 0.364 MRR, random 0.369, the constraint solver 0.706; humans preferred Holodeck over ProcTHOR 64.4% on 120 rooms ([Holodeck](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.09067)) **[docs]**. Successors keep the design and win again: Scenethesis 85% preference over Holodeck, collisions 0.8% vs 6.1% ([Scenethesis](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02836)); HSM 76.6% fidelity ([HSM](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.16848v3)) **[docs]**. Holodeck itself notes it "struggles with scenes like restaurants" (a tavern is one) and costs ~3 min and ~0.20 USD per room on GPT-4 **[docs]**. None target Blender; the pattern ports, the code does not.
3. **Asset pool, free and scriptable today**: Poly Haven (521 CC0 PBR models — props 176, furniture 85, containers 68; `/search?q=medieval tavern barrel` returns `wine_barrel_01`, `barrel_03`, `bar_chair_round_01`, `gothic_coffee_table`; no key) **[live]**; BlenderKit (23,962 free models, 2,121 hits for "medieval tavern", 4,070 CC0; API key for downloads; Full plan 8.90 USD/month for 82,473) **[live/docs]**; stylised low-poly CC0: Quaternius Medieval Village MegaKit 304 + Fantasy Props MegaKit 211, Kenney Castle 75 / Furniture 140 / Fantasy Town 160, scriptable through Poly Pizza (10,700+ GLBs) **[docs]**. Realistic and stylised pools do not mix visually; choose per comic.
4. **Rank 2 route (gap-filling): image-to-3D per prop.** Open: **TRELLIS.2-4B** (MIT, PBR base/rough/metal, ≥24 GB VRAM, H100 ~17 s at 1024³ → ~0.02 USD; user preference 66.5% vs Hunyuan3D 2.1 13.3%) ([TRELLIS.2](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2), [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.14692v1)) **[docs/est]**. Hosted: **Tripo H3.1** at 0.30 USD per PBR prop (+0.05 quad, `auto_size`, `delight`) ([Tripo pricing](https://docs.tripo3d.ai/get-started/pricing.html)) **[docs]**. **Every Tencent Hunyuan3D community licence (2.0, 2.1, Omni, Part) and the HunyuanWorld licences exclude the EU, UK and South Korea outright** ([2.1 LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1/main/LICENSE)) **[docs]**; the workstation locale is Spanish, so if the team is in the EU the open Hunyuan weights are not an option, research or not **[est]**; the hosted Tencent API (0.50–0.70 USD per PBR prop, 200 free credits, a **Sketch** line-art mode) remains.
5. **Painterly comic cards break image-to-3D in documented ways**: the only quantified painting test (Pixal3D, 11 objects from 9 paintings) fused the background into a slab in 5/7, bled colour in 3/4 textures and baked the painting's perspective into the geometry ([MindrLabs report](https://github.com/MindrLabs/image-to-3d-pixal3d)) **[secondary, measured]**. Mitigations that exist: segment first (our cards already have alpha), `delight`/`remove_lighting` (removes shading, not ink lines), canonical-pose models for reusable props vs Pixal3D for the drawn viewpoint. Characters stay 2D cards (CONTEXT.md: cards cannot walk); props are the target.
6. **Procedural, free, headless-safe for the outdoor box**: Blender 5.2's bundled Geometry Nodes assets (Scatter on Surface, Array, Curve to Tube, SDF grid → Grid to Mesh for blobby cloud meshes) appended from `datafiles/assets/nodes/geometry_nodes_essentials.blend`; A.N.T. Landscape and Terrain Mixer (GPL, `blender --command extension install`) for the valley; Easy Tree (GPL) for vegetation ([5.0 GN notes](https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/5.0/geometry_nodes/), [extensions CLI](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/dev/advanced/command_line/extension_arguments.html)) **[docs]**. Paid and worth it: Medieval House Generator (29 USD, Blender 5.2+, Realize → one mesh, 2K hand-painted textures) for exteriors **[docs]**. Infinigen (BSD-3, 2.0.0a5 on 2026-11-05) is photoreal contemporary rooms in 8–13 CPU-minutes, no tavern vocabulary, no glTF export **[docs]**.
7. **Comic look that survives GLB**: Shader-to-RGB is EEVEE-only and not bakeable; Toon BSDF is Cycles-only; baking is Cycles-only ([EEVEE node support](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/eevee/limitations/nodes_support.html), [Cycles baking](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/cycles/baking.html)) **[docs]**. The route that works headless: bake Diffuse Color + Diffuse Direct, quantise the lighting in Python, multiply into one albedo, export as `KHR_materials_unlit` (glTFast and three.js both honour it) plus inverted-hull outlines as plain mesh. Goo Engine (stuck at 4.4), Malt (render-only), Lightning Boy (3.4-only) are dead ends for export **[docs]**.
8. **Blender as a service**: `bpy==5.2.3` wheel exists for **Python 3.13 only** (403 MB; no 3.14 wheel for any version) ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/pypi/bpy/json)) **[docs]**, so the Blender worker runs in `uv run --python 3.13` beside the 3.14 pipeline. The ahujasid add-on refuses `blender -b` by design (timers never fire) ([addon.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahujasid/blender-mcp/main/addon.py)) **[docs]**; own the loop in a plain `import bpy` process instead. Cycles GPU needs no display; EEVEE headless needs NVIDIA EGL, which Modal does not document **[unverified]**. Modal L4 at 0.80 USD/h renders a 1200×500 Cycles preview at 64 spp in ~3–8 s ≈ 0.0015 USD; a warm L4 session is ~0.93 USD/h, inside the 30 USD/month free credit for ~30 session-hours ([Modal pricing](https://modal.com/pricing)) **[docs/est]**.
9. **Cost per setting** with the recommended architecture: ~0.3–0.6 USD when gap props come from TRELLIS.2 on Modal, ~1.5–2.5 USD when 3–5 come from Tripo; assets and procedural pieces are free **[est]**. Both fit "a few USD per episode" for 3–6 settings only if the generated share stays small.
10. **Recommendation**: build the retrieval + constraint-layout + bake route first (free assets, one weekend of engineering), with TRELLIS.2 on an L40S as the gap-filler, and run the 5 USD experiment in §"Cheapest path" on the taproom and the sky box before buying any add-on.

## 1. Agentic Blender tooling

### What exists and what it achieves

- **ahujasid/mcp-for-blender** (renamed from blender-mcp 2026-09-16): MIT, 33.6k stars, last commit 2026-11-04; 9 tools: `execute_blender_code`, `look`, `get_scene_info`, `generate_3d` (Tripo, Hunyuan3D, Hyper3D Rodin, Meshy), `search_assets`/`import_asset` (Poly Haven ~2,400 CC0 assets no key; Sketchfab with key; Poly Pizza ~10,600 low-poly, free key), telemetry on by default ([README](https://github.com/ahujasid/mcp-for-blender)) **[docs]**. Limits it states: Poly Haven downloads block the main thread; Poly Pizza is Cloudflare-blocked from datacenter IPs (relevant to Modal); complex requests time out. Evidence of quality: demo videos only. Its value for us is the retrieval and generative plumbing, not the code execution.
- **Blender Lab official MCP server** (the `mcp__blender__*` tools in this session: `execute_blender_code`, `get_blendfile_summary_*`, `*_for_cli`, `render_viewport_to_path`, `search_api_docs`): GPL-3, v1.0.0 2026-04-27, add-on v1.0.3 2026-09-11, requires Blender 5.1+; 26 tools; the six `_for_cli` variants open a .blend in a background Blender, which is the headless path; "will execute LLM generated code in Blender without any guards" ([blender.org/lab/mcp-server](https://www.blender.org/lab/mcp-server/)) **[docs]**. No asset libraries, no generative 3D.
- Community servers are bpy wrappers at scale: blenderwright (MIT, 276 tools, 5.2-only, author claims a saloon in ~20 min **[unverified]**), meshwire (AGPL, 225 tools, render → Ollama-vision score → refine loop, local TripoSR/SF3D/Hunyuan3D), mcp-blender-agent (MIT, ~216 typed ops), carlosh7/blender-mcp (MIT, Poly Haven + Sketchfab + ambientCG + Hyper3D) **[docs]**. Skill packs: arjun988/blender-skills (MIT, 2.1k stars, 94 skills, needs ahujasid MCP), RobLe3/cc-blender-skill (MIT, 30 skills, author: "design quality… not solved"), majidmanzarpour/blender-game-skills (MIT, silhouette-IoU evidence gates, 5.2) **[docs]**. None shows a rendered result better than primitives.
- **Research systems, with numbers**:
  - *Holodeck* (CVPR 2024, Apache-2.0, dormant since 2024-08): GPT-4 proposes floor plan, materials (236), objects; retrieves from a 51,464-asset curated Objaverse subset by 100×CLIP + 1×SBERT − 10×size discrepancy; GPT-4 emits constraints (against wall, on top of, facing); DFS/MILP solver 30 s. Preferred over ProcTHOR 64.4% overall, 59.8% assets, 56.9% layout; ~3 min and ~0.20 USD per room; outputs AI2-THOR JSON, "struggles with scenes like restaurants" ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.09067), [repo](https://github.com/allenai/Holodeck)) **[docs]**.
  - *SceneCraft* (ICML 2024, no code): scene graph → constraint functions → bpy → GPT-4V critique loop; CLIP 69.8 vs BlenderGPT 24.7; human wins 76.8% fidelity, 83.6% composition, 74.5% aesthetics; assets from Turbosquid ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.01248)) **[docs]**.
  - *Scenethesis* (NVIDIA, ICLR 2026, no code found): LLM layout → generated image guidance → vision structure → physics optimisation; CLIP 30.71 vs Holodeck 28.32, preference 85%, collisions 0.8% vs 6.1% ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02836)) **[docs]**. *HSM*: 76.6% fidelity vs Holodeck, ~8 min and ~0.50 USD per scene ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.16848v3)) **[docs]**.
  - *LayoutGPT*: layout only, 43–74% of rooms still have out-of-bound furniture ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.15393)) **[docs]**. *3D-GPT*: picks Infinigen nature functions, outdoor only, repo stale ([repo](https://github.com/Chuny1/3DGPT)) **[docs]**. *BlenderGPT*: last commit 2023-06-10, the baseline everyone beats ([repo](https://github.com/gd3kr/BlenderGPT)) **[docs]**. *BlenderAlchemy*: VLM edit search over materials/lighting on existing scenes, pinned to an old Infinigen ([repo](https://github.com/ianhuang0630/BlenderAlchemyOfficial)) **[docs]**.
  - *Benchmarks*: BlenderGym (CVPR 2025): human blend-shape loss 0.934e-3 vs GPT-4o 9.14e-3; verifier scaling helps ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01786)). 3DHarnessBench (2026): giving agents geometric queries (bounding boxes, part stats) helps every agent, best Uni3D 0.927 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06535v1)) **[docs]**. 3DCodeBench's top failure: Blender 5.0 API mismatches from stale training data, so pin a 5.2 API sheet in the agent's prompt (5.2 changed GN modifier inputs to `modifier.properties.inputs.<id>.value`) ([5.2 Python API notes](https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/5.2/python_api/)) **[docs]**.
- **Panorama/world generators**: HunyuanWorld 1.0 and HY-World 2.0 (panorama → layered mesh / 3DGS; Lite runs on a 4090) have the same EU/UK/KR territory exclusion ([LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanWorld-1.0/main/LICENSE)) **[docs]**. World Labs Marble exports splats on all paid plans and a textured GLB only on Pro; API 1,500 credits (1.20 USD) per world, HQ GLB 3,500 (2.80 USD) ([API pricing](https://docs.worldlabs.ai/api/pricing)) **[docs]**. Meta's WorldGen (procedural blockout → AssetGen2 → part decomposition, ~5 min) is the closest to "editable set from one prompt" but has no release ([paper](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2511.16825)) **[docs]**. These suit the `panorama` tier, not props.

### Settled

Retrieval + relational layout is the route with human-study wins; primitives from code is benchmarked toy-like; procedural is photoreal inside its vocabulary only. The agent must output constraints and asset queries, be given measurements (bounding boxes, floor contact) not only screenshots, and work on a Blender 5.2 API sheet.

### NPR that survives GLB export

Blender facts: Shader to RGB is EEVEE-only; Toon BSDF, Hair BSDF, Sheen are not in EEVEE ([node support](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/eevee/limitations/nodes_support.html)); baking is Cycles-only with Combined, Diffuse (Color/Direct/Indirect), Emit, Glossy, Normal, AO, Position, UV ([baking](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/cycles/baking.html)); Grease Pencil Line Art can be baked to strokes ([Line Art](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/grease_pencil/modifiers/generate/line_art.html)); the glTF exporter writes `KHR_materials_unlit` when the material mixes Emission and Transparent on Light Path *Is Camera* with no Principled, exports vertex colours as COLOR_0, and drops curves ([glTF add-on](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/import_export/scene_gltf2.html)) **[docs]**. Kuwahara exists only as a compositor node ([manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/dev/compositing/types/creative/kuwahara.html)) **[docs]**.

Ranked routes:

1. **Bake-to-unlit** (headless, zero runtime cost on Quest 3): Cycles bake Diffuse Color and Diffuse Direct per object → quantise the lighting image to 2–4 bands in Python (or Kuwahara via a compositor pass) → multiply into one albedo → Emission + Light Path mix → `KHR_materials_unlit`. glTFast 7.0 imports and exports unlit ([features](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.cloud.gltfast@7.0/manual/features.html)); three.js maps it to `MeshBasicMaterial` ([GLTFLoader](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/jsm/loaders/GLTFLoader.js)) **[docs]**.
2. **Baked albedo + engine toon**: export Principled Base Color; the reader bands at runtime (three.js `MeshToonMaterial` with a NearestFilter `gradientMap` ([source](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/src/materials/MeshToonMaterial.js)); Unity URP: NiloCat toon, MIT ([repo](https://github.com/ColinLeung-NiloCat/UnityURPToonLitShaderExample))) **[docs]**. Keeps lighting dynamic; costs a shader on Quest, and outline passes are "not recommended for mobile VR" **[secondary]**.
3. **Outlines as mesh**: inverted hull (Solidify + flipped normals, view-independent, exports as plain mesh) preferred over Line Art → Grease Pencil to Curves → Curve to Mesh (camera-dependent) **[docs/est]**.
4. **AI repaint of albedo in the comic's style**: SyncMVD (MIT, SD 1.5 + depth/normal ControlNets, ≤~40k tris) ([repo](https://github.com/LIU-Yuxin/SyncMVD)); Paint3D (Apache-2.0, 2K UV albedo) ([repo](https://github.com/OpenTexture/Paint3D)); Hunyuan3D-2.1 Paint (~21 GB, EU-excluded licence); Meshy Retexture 10 credits ([pricing](https://docs.meshy.ai/api/pricing)) **[docs]**. Dream Textures is stale (0.4.1, 2024-08) and StableProjectorz is a Windows GUI (AGPL since 2026-01) **[docs]**.

Dead ends for export: Shader-to-RGB trees, Toon BSDF (Diffuse bake loses the banding), Goo Engine (Patreon builds, 4.4, "harder and harder" past EEVEE Legacy ([Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/posts/goo-engine-4-3-2-141482680))), Malt (render engine, 5.0 build 2026-03) and BEER (2021), Lightning Boy Shader (3.4 only), Komikaze (EEVEE shaders, no baking docs), Freestyle (image only) **[docs]**.

What must happen in Unity, not Blender: any dynamic banding, rim light or screen-space outline; the material swap on load if route 2 is used; billboarding and alpha sorting if clouds are textured cards; `EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing` or realised instances; AO baked into albedo if the look needs it.

## Recommendation: quality per dollar and per engineering hour

1. **Retrieval + relational layout + bake (Rank 1).** Quality is the only one with human-study wins (64–85% preference in the Holodeck line); assets are free (Poly Haven CC0, BlenderKit free tier, Quaternius/Kenney CC0); the per-setting spend is LLM calls (~0.2–0.4 USD, anchored on Holodeck's 0.20 USD per room with GPT-4 **[docs]**, more with VLM critiques) and ~5–10 Modal L4 minutes (~0.08–0.15 USD) **[est]**. Engineering: a planner prompt that emits an asset query list and constraints, a bounding-box constraint solver (annealing over floor contact, wall adjacency, facing, no overlap; Holodeck's solver runs 30 s), an importer that normalises scale and orientation (Objaverse-class assets are "uploaded at unnatural scales"), the bake-to-unlit pass, and the preview-critique loop with a tool-call cap. 2–4 days to a first tavern **[est]**. Its limit is the pool: a prop the libraries lack cannot appear.
2. **Image-to-3D gap-fill (Rank 2).** TRELLIS.2 on an L40S at 1024³ costs cents per prop plus a cold start (the 4B weights), is MIT with no territory clause, and gives PBR GLBs with a decimation target we set; Tripo H3.1 at 0.30–0.35 USD per PBR quad prop needs no subscription and ships `auto_size` and `delight`. Use for the 2–5 props per setting the pool lacks (a tavern sign, a specific cauldron, a broom), from a text-to-image card drawn in the comic's style or from the panel's own card with its alpha. Untested on comic cards; the painterly failure modes are documented, so expect a repaint pass. One day to wire either **[est]**.
3. **Procedural (Rank 3)** for the outdoor box: SDF-grid cloud meshes, A.N.T./Terrain Mixer mountains, Easy Tree, all GPL and CLI-installable, deterministic and free; the Medieval House Generator (29 USD) for the castle and the tavern exterior. 1–2 days **[est]**.
4. **Not now**: Infinigen (contemporary vocabulary, no glTF), world generators (backdrop only, Tencent licences void in the EU, Marble GLB 2.80 USD and splats), Objaverse at scale (TBs and an index to build), paid scatter tools (Geo-Scatter forbids scripting below Studio), and any further primitives-from-code tooling.

## Architecture for the props step

Input: `episode.settings[]` from step 10 (id, name, description, indoor, timeOfDay, weather) plus the erased backdrops of that setting's frames and the comic's style preset. Output: one `set.glb` per setting (a schema addition; today `scenePath` only matches `panels/<id>/scene.glb`, which holds the frame windows and layer cards) **[in-house]**. Characters remain 2D cards in `scene.glb`; the set sits behind and around the frame windows.

1. **Set brief** (one Claude Sonnet 5.5 call per setting, text + the setting's backdrop contact sheet): room envelope (floor polygon, wall height, openings), palette sampled from the backdrops, a **style pool decision** (realistic Poly Haven/BlenderKit vs low-poly Quaternius/Kenney), a prop list with counts, and **relational constraints** per prop (against wall N, on top of X, facing Y, cluster of 4 around a table), never coordinates. Also an **HDRI/lighting brief** (indoor 301 Poly Haven HDRIs; warm, candles) and a **generate list** for props the pool is unlikely to hold.
2. **Retrieval**: Poly Haven `/search` then `/files`; BlenderKit `search/?query=<kw>+asset_type:model+is_free:true` filtered on `faceCount` ≤ 20k and `modelStyle`; local CC0 kit by CLIP on thumbnails. Cache in a Modal Volume keyed by source id. Each import is normalised: apply transforms, largest dimension to the brief's size, floor at z=0, front axis by the asset's longest horizontal extent (verified by one VLM glance when ambiguous).
3. **Generation for the gap list**: a style card per prop (the comic's style preset through the existing image route, flat background, 3/4 view) → TRELLIS.2 at 1024³ on an L40S (batch the episode's props in one container) → decimate to ≤ 20k tris, 2048² textures; fallback Tripo H3.1 `pbr=true, quad=true, auto_size=true, delight=true`. Budget cap: 5 generated props per setting.
4. **Layout solve** in Python (not the LLM): bounding boxes on the floor polygon, hard constraints (inside room, no overlap, floor contact, wall adjacency within 5 cm, clearance in front of seats), soft constraints (facing, grouping, symmetry), simulated annealing, 10–30 s; then a second pass for small objects on surfaces (HSM's motif idea: mugs on tables, bottles on the bar).
5. **Compose in Blender 5.2.3 on Modal L4** (`import bpy` worker, own loop): append GLBs, place by the solved transforms, build walls/floor/ceiling from the envelope with Poly Haven wood/plaster/brick textures, GN Array for beams and booths, Scatter on Surface for straw or clutter, HDRI + candle emitters.
6. **Stylise**: bake Diffuse Color and Diffuse Direct per object at 1024–2048², quantise the lighting to 3 bands with the palette's warm bias, multiply, optional Kuwahara, add inverted-hull outlines where the comic uses ink; or, if the comic is painterly-realistic, skip quantisation and keep PBR.
7. **Critique loop**: Cycles 1200×500 at 64 spp + OIDN (~3–8 s) from 2–3 cameras including the frame's camera; Claude Haiku 5.5 scores composition, scale, overlap, palette, and emits constraint edits; cap 4 rounds; also feed it the solver's numeric report (overlaps, out-of-bounds, floor gaps) as 3DHarnessBench suggests.
8. **Export**: `export_scene.gltf(GLB, JPEG/PNG textures, Draco on, export_apply=True)`, textures ≤ 2048², target ≤ 30–50 MB for Quest 3; unlit materials via `KHR_materials_unlit`; write `settings/<id>/set.glb` and a `set.json` with the brief, the asset provenance (source, id, licence, attribution if CC-BY) and the solved layout for re-runs.

Cost per setting **[est]**: LLM 0.2–0.4 USD (brief + 4 critiques), Modal L4 5–10 min 0.08–0.15 USD, generated props 0.02–0.07 USD each on TRELLIS.2 plus one cold start per episode (~1–2 min L40S, ~0.05 USD) or 0.35 USD each on Tripo. Totals: ~0.3–0.6 USD per setting with open generation, ~1.5–2.5 with 3–5 Tripo props. An episode with 4 settings: ~1.5–2.5 USD open, 6–10 USD hosted; the hosted variant breaks the per-episode budget unless generation is rare.

## Open questions

- EU status: the Tencent licences exclude the EU outright; confirm the team's jurisdiction before touching Hunyuan3D or HunyuanWorld weights. The hosted Tencent API's own terms for EU users were not read.
- EEVEE on Modal: whether the runtime exposes `libEGL_nvidia`; if not, Cycles at 64 spp is the preview engine (3–8 s on L4 [est], unmeasured).
- BlenderKit's `blenderkit_download` operator under `blender -b` and its local Go client; the plain download URL with the API key is the fallback but the exact header form (`api_key` param vs `Authorization: Bearer`) is only attested second-hand.
- Poly Pizza's API docs are JS-rendered and its CDN is Cloudflare-fronted; whether Modal egress IPs are blocked is untested.
- Sketchfab download with a personal token (no end-user OAuth) is undocumented in practice.
- No published benchmark covers image-to-3D on flat-colour outlined comic art; Route B's 8-prop side-by-side is the only measurement we will have. Nothing published removes ink outlines from the texture.
- TRELLIS.2's real time and VRAM on an L40S (only H100 figures are published); whether 24 GB cards work at 1024³.
- Tripo's free API credits (300 vs 2,000 claimed by third parties) and Meshy's USD credit price were not verifiable from the vendors' pages today.
- glTFast and `EXT_texture_webp`: not listed; JPEG/PNG assumed. Quest 3 GLB size limits are practice, not documentation.
- The `.zumi` schema has no `settings[].setPath`; where the set GLB lives and how `ZumiPanelScene` composes it with the frame windows (stencils, scale at distance 1) is a design decision for the step itself.
- Holodeck-class systems admit restaurants are hard; whether a 100-line annealer with booth and bar constraints gets a taproom right is the first thing Route A measures.
