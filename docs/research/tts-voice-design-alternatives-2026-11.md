# TTS alternatives to Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS voice design — 2026-11-06

Research date: 2026-11-06. Official sources only (vendor docs, pricing and rate-limit pages, model cards, LICENSE files), read by five parallel agents; secondary sources are named as such. Builds on `tts-voices-2026-10.md` (quality leaderboards, Meta, cost per audio-hour) and does not repeat it.

Conventions: **[docs]** = copied from the cited page. **[est]** = my arithmetic from [docs] figures, or an inference. **[in-house]** = observed by our pipeline (`pipeline/segment/gemini.py`), not from a doc. A "4 s line" is ~60 characters; a 4 s line on token-billed APIs is ~100 audio tokens at 25 tokens/s. Load: ~70 lines per episode, 12 episodes now (840 lines), target capacity ≥500 lines/day.

## Summary (ranked for our case)

1. Why Gemini blocks us: Tier 1 is 10 requests/min and 100/day on the TTS model **[in-house, 429 messages 2026-11-05/06]**; Google no longer publishes per-model tables (the page says "view in AI Studio") **[docs]**; Tier 2 needs 100 USD cumulative paid + 3 days **[docs]**. Designed voices are per project, 200 per project, 1-year sliding TTL **[docs]**.
2. **Inworld Realtime TTS-2** is the closest drop-in: `voices:design` → `:publish` gives a permanent `voiceId`; the delivery note goes in verbatim as a free-form `[say in a tired, whiny, sulking way]` tag; On-Demand plan is 0 USD/month with 5 concurrent synth calls and no RPM/RPD; 25 USD per 1M chars = 0.0015 USD per line (Flash: 0.0009, but Flash ignores instruction tags); 70 min free **[docs]**. Cost for all 840 lines: ~1.3 USD **[est]**.
3. **Self-hosting on Modal** removes rate limits entirely and costs ~0.3–0.6 USD/day at 500 lines **[est]**: VoxCPM2 (Apache-2.0, 8 GB VRAM, description prefix for design and style prefix per line) or Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign → clone prompt (Apache-2.0; the clone model loses per-line instruct) paired with IndexTTS-2 `emo_text` or CosyVoice 3 `inference_instruct2` for the delivery note. Engineering cost: a Modal service and a persistence layer (design once, keep the WAV, clone every line).
4. **Fish Audio** is the cheapest hosted route (s2.1-pro 15 USD/1M bytes = 0.0009 per line; `s2.1-pro-free` at 0 USD; voice design 0.01 USD per call; free-form `[bracket]` cues; 5 concurrent) but the designed voice is stateless and must be persisted by cloning the chosen candidate into a `reference_id` (chaining not documented) and the terms pages 404 **[docs]**.
5. **ElevenLabs Starter** (6 USD/month) is the quality option: design → `voice_id`, free-form audio tags, 3–6 concurrent, no daily cap; 0.0024 USD/line on v4 Turbo, 0.0048 on v4/v3; only 10 voice slots on Starter **[docs]**.
6. **Gemini remains cheapest** (0.0009 USD/line, 0.00045 batch) if Tier 2 is reached; the tier is per billing account on cumulative Google Cloud spend, so a 100 USD prepayment may unlock it (whether prepaid credit counts as "paid" is unverified) **[docs/est]**.
7. Rejected on facts: Hume (TTS API ends 2026-11-13, voices not exportable); PlayHT/Play.ai (service gone, domains do not resolve); OpenAI (prompt-to-voice exists but only in GPT-Live, not on `/v1/audio/speech`; gpt-4o-mini-tts shuts down 2027-01-06); Cartesia, Azure, Cloud TTS Chirp 3, Polly (no voice design from text; Azure/Cloud custom voices need consented human recordings and allow-listing).
8. MiniMax works (voice_design → `voice_id`, 3 USD per voice) but per-line delivery is a 9-value enum, no whisper on 2.8, and 0.0036–0.006 USD/line.
9. Open models with text voice design and a permissive licence: Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign, VoxCPM2, MOSS-VoiceGenerator, Maya1 (all Apache-2.0); non-commercial but fine for research: Breeze TTS 2, OmniVoice. None exposes a reusable "designed voice" object; all persist by design-once-then-clone.
10. Recommendation: switch the hosted path to Inworld TTS-2 now (zero plan cost, one afternoon of integration: two endpoints change, tag syntax replaces `speech_metadata.style`); in parallel run the 1 USD test in §"Cheapest path" on Inworld and VoxCPM2 and keep Gemini voices as the reference for the A/B.

## Hosted APIs

### Gemini API (gemini-3.8-flash-tts, gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts)

- Voice design: `POST /v1beta/voices` with `store: true`, `type: "prompted"`, `prompted.input` = description; returns permanent `voice_…` id and `sample_audio` **[docs]** https://aistudio.google.com/docs/voice-design . Only on the 3.8 Flash / Flash-Lite TTS models **[docs]**. Stored per project: 200 stateful voices, 1-year sliding TTL from last use; `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` beyond that **[docs]** https://aistudio.google.com/docs/speech-generation . No separate quota or price for the voices endpoint is published **[docs, absence]**. Remixing: same endpoint with `prompted.base_voice` **[docs]** https://aistudio.google.com/docs/voice-remixing .
- Per line: `annotations.speech_metadata.style` free text; inline `<short pause>`, `<long pause>`, `<laugh>`, `<sigh>`, CAPITALS; docs warn long persona blocks in `style` "increase drift" and permanent traits belong in the design **[docs]**. Multi-speaker (≤2) is prebuilt voices only **[docs]**. No SSML.
- Rate limits: the rate-limits page no longer lists per-model RPM/RPD; "can be viewed in Google AI Studio" **[docs]** https://aistudio.google.com/docs/rate-limits . Observed on our Tier 1 project: 10 requests/min and 100/day on the TTS model **[in-house]**. Tiers: Tier 1 = billing linked (250 USD/month cap, 10 USD per 10 min); Tier 2 = 100 USD cumulative paid + 3 days since first payment (2,000 USD cap); Tier 3 = 1,000 USD + 30 days; spend counts "total cumulative spending on Google Cloud services for the billing account"; minimum prepay 5 USD **[docs]** https://aistudio.google.com/docs/billing . Batch mode has its own limits (100 concurrent jobs, 24 h target), listed only for 2.5 TTS (enqueued tokens 100,000 at T1) **[docs]** https://aistudio.google.com/docs/batch-mode .
- Price: 0.50 USD/1M text in, 9.00 USD/1M audio out through 2026-12-31, then 1.00 / 18.00; batch and Flex half; Flash-Lite 6.00 (then 12.00); 25 audio tokens per second **[docs]** https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing . 4 s line ≈ 0.0009 USD (Flash), 0.0006 (Lite), 0.00045 (batch) **[est]**. 2.5 and 3.1 preview TTS models: earliest shutdown 2026-11-17 **[docs]** https://aistudio.google.com/docs/deprecations .
- Terms: Google claims no ownership of output; paid-tier data not used to improve products; services are "for developers … professional or business purposes" **[docs]** https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms .
- Quality: WAV 24 kHz 16-bit mono; 8,192 in / 16,384 out tokens; 130+ languages **[docs]**.

### Inworld Realtime TTS-2 (`inworld-tts-2`, `inworld-tts-2-flash`)

- Design: `POST https://api.inworld.ai/voices/v1/voices:design` with `designPrompt` (English, ≤1000 chars; "<30 characters rarely produce a useful voice"), `previewText` (shapes the voice, 50–400 chars), `languageCode`, `designPromptMode` ASSISTED|VERBATIM, `numberOfSamples` 1–3 → preview `voiceId`s in DRAFT **[docs]** https://docs.inworld.ai/api-reference/voiceAPI/voiceservice/design-voice.md . Persist: `POST /voices/v1/voices/{voiceId}:publish` → stable `voiceId` **[docs]** https://docs.inworld.ai/api-reference/voiceAPI/voiceservice/publish-voice.md ; guide https://docs.inworld.ai/tts/voice-design.md . Ad-hoc `voiceDesign.designPrompt` per request exists but "does not guarantee the same voice across requests" **[docs]**. Custom-voice cap: 100 on On-Demand (designed + cloned) **[docs]** https://inworld.ai/pricing .
- Per line (TTS-2 only; Flash ignores instructions, keeps non-verbals): inline `[natural-language instruction]` before the words, persists until changed or `[reset]`; examples `[say with force]`, `[whisper in a hushed style]`, `[say sadly]`, `[very slow]`; non-verbals `[laugh]`, `[sigh]`, `[yawn]`… Instructions must be English even for other languages **[docs]** https://docs.inworld.ai/tts/capabilities/steering.md . Also `synthesisContext.reasoning: REASONING_ON` + `reasoningContext` (an LLM writes the instruction, not billed), `previousRequests` for continuity, `audioConfig.speakingRate` 0.5–1.5, `deliveryMode` STABLE|BALANCED|CREATIVE **[docs]** https://docs.inworld.ai/api-reference/ttsAPI/texttospeech/synthesize-speech.md .
- Limits: On-Demand (0 USD/month, pay as you go) 5 concurrent TTS; voice design/clone 2 per minute; no RPM/RPD; raised only by plan **[docs]** https://docs.inworld.ai/resources/rate-limits.md , https://docs.inworld.ai/portal/billing.md .
- Price: TTS-2 25 USD/1M chars On-Demand (20 Creator at 25 USD/month); Flash 15; batch API 20% less; 70 min TTS free on On-Demand **[docs]** https://inworld.ai/pricing . Price per voice design not listed **[docs, absence]**. 4 s line 0.0015 USD; 500 lines/day 0.75 USD; 840 lines 1.26 USD **[est]**.
- Terms: "Commercial license" on On-Demand; outputs assigned to the user; "solely for your internal business purposes" **[docs]** https://inworld.ai/terms .
- Quality: 200+ languages (15 Tier 1); text ≤2,000 UTF-16 units per sync request; output up to 48 kHz "capped at the model's native rate" (native rate not stated); word timestamps **[docs]** https://docs.inworld.ai/tts/capabilities/generating-audio.md . TTS-1 discontinued 2026-06-15, TTS-1.5 deprecated **[docs]**. Earlier note: AA Elo 1259 (2026-10-15).

### Fish Audio (hosted; s2.1-pro, s2-pro, drama-3, s2.1-pro-free; s1 retires 2026-12-31)

- Design: `POST https://api.fish.audio/v1/voice-design`, header `model: voice-design-1`; `instruction` 1–2000 chars, `reference_text` (preview line, ≤150 or ≤300 chars — two pages disagree), `n` 1–4, `seed` (deterministic), `num_step`, `guidance_scale`, `instruct_guidance_scale`; returns `candidates[]` with WAV base64. "Stateless API call … without creating … voice models" **[docs]** https://docs.fish.audio/api-reference/endpoint/openapi-v1/voice-design.md , https://docs.fish.audio/features/voice-design.md . Persist by `POST /model` (`type: tts`, `voices[]`, `texts[]`, `train_mode: fast`) → `_id` used as `reference_id`; the docs show this only for uploaded audio, so feeding a designed candidate is my chaining **[est]** https://docs.fish.audio/developer-guide/core-features/creating-models.md . Web-app voice design is excluded from the Free plan **[docs]**.
- Per line (s2 family): free-form square-bracket cues, "not limited to a fixed set": `[happy]`, `[sad][whispering]`, `[slightly sad]`, `[warm and happy]`; ≤3 combined, sentence-level, not billed, all languages **[docs]** https://docs.fish.audio/developer-guide/core-features/emotions.md . s1 used a fixed `(parenthesis)` set (49 emotions, 5 tones incl. shouting/whispering, 10 effects) **[docs]** https://docs.fish.audio/developer-guide/models-pricing/migrate-from-s1.md . Numeric: `prosody.speed` 0.5–2, `prosody.volume`, `temperature`, `top_p`, `chunk_length` **[docs]**.
- Limits: concurrency by prepaid total: <100 USD → 5 concurrent; ≥100 → 15; ≥1,000 → 50; no RPM; 429 without Retry-After **[docs]** https://docs.fish.audio/developer-guide/models-pricing/pricing-and-rate-limits.md .
- Price: s2.1-pro / s2-pro / s1 15 USD per 1M UTF-8 bytes; drama-3 50 (10 promo to 2026-11-30); `s2.1-pro-free` 0 USD with fair-use limits; voice-design-1 0.01 USD per successful request; clone model creation not priced **[docs]**. 4 s line 0.0009 USD; 500/day 0.45 USD; 10 designed voices 0.10 USD **[est]**.
- Terms: fish.audio/terms-of-service, /legal/terms and /pricing returned 404 **[docs, unverified]**.
- Quality: s2.1-pro 83 languages; wav/pcm 8–44.1 kHz, mp3, opus 48 kHz; no stated max text length **[docs]** https://docs.fish.audio/developer-guide/models-pricing/models-overview.md .

### ElevenLabs (Eleven v4 / v4 Turbo / v3)

- Design: `POST /v1/text-to-voice/design` (`voice_description`, `model_id` `eleven_multilingual_ttv_v2` or `eleven_ttv_v3`, `text` 100–1000 chars or `auto_generate_text`, `guidance_scale`, `seed`, `loudness`, `reference_audio_base64` + `prompt_strength` on ttv_v3) → 3 previews; `POST /v1/text-to-voice/create` with `generated_voice_id` → permanent `voice_id` **[docs]** https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-voice/design , https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-voice/create . Voice slots: Free 3, Starter 10, Creator 30, Pro 160 **[docs]** https://elevenlabs.io/pricing . Docs call Voice Design "still experimental" and say designed voices "may be less performative" on v4 than v3 **[docs]** https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-design.md .
- Per line: inline audio tags in `text`, free-form (`[whispers]`, `[sighs]`, `[sarcastic]`, `[shouting]`, `[low, gravelly voice]`; "not a complete list"); tags land only when the delivery is in the voice's training data; no SSML `<break>` on v3 **[docs]** https://elevenlabs.io/docs/best-practices/prompting/eleven-v3 . `voice_settings.stability`, `speed` 0.7–1.2, `previous_text`/`next_text`, `previous_request_ids` (≤3) for continuity **[docs]** https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-speech/convert .
- Limits: concurrency only ("requests per minute matter less"): Starter 3 (v2/v3/v4 class) or 6 (Flash class) per the models page; the API pricing page shows Starter 6; no daily cap; scales by plan **[docs]** https://elevenlabs.io/docs/models .
- Price: API per character on every plan: v4 / v3 / Multilingual v2 0.08 USD per 1K; v4 Turbo / v3 Conversational / Flash 0.04 **[docs]** https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/api . Starter 6 USD/month (first month 1 USD), ~75,000 chars v4-class or ~150,000 Turbo-class per month; the general pricing page says 30,000 credits — the two pages disagree **[docs]**. Free 10,000 chars/month, non-commercial. Voice design consumes credits equal to the preview text once for all three previews **[docs]** https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/voices/voice-design/how-much-does-voice-design-cost.md . 4 s line 0.0024 (Turbo-class) / 0.0048 (v4/v3); 500 lines/day 1.20 / 2.40 USD **[est]**.
- Terms: Free plan non-commercial only; paid plans commercial; "you retain all rights in and to your Output" **[docs]** https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use .
- Quality: v4 90+ languages, 10,000 chars/request; v3 70+, 5,000; MP3 44.1 kHz default, PCM 44.1 kHz needs Pro+; `seed` best-effort only **[docs]**. Earlier note: AA Elo 1326–1328.

- Limits: T2A 60 RPM, voice design 20 RPM, no concurrency/daily cap listed; audio packages: Starter 5 USD = 10 RPM, Standard 30 USD = 50 RPM **[docs]** https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/rate-limits , https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/pricing-speech.md .

## Cheapest path to test the top 2

Total expected cost under 1 USD; both tests reuse the same 3 character descriptions and 20 lines with delivery notes already produced for Gemini, so Gemini output is the reference.

Inworld TTS-2 (expected 0 USD: inside the 70 free minutes) **[est]**:
1. Create an account on the On-Demand plan (0 USD/month); get an API key.
2. For each of 3 characters: `POST /voices/v1/voices:design` with `designPrompt` = our reader-model description (pad to ≥30 chars, English), `previewText` = one of that character's real lines (50–400 chars), `numberOfSamples: 3`. Pace at 2 per minute (the design limit).
3. Listen to the 3 previews, `POST /voices/v1/voices/{voiceId}:publish` the best; store the `voiceId` where `gemini.py` stores the `voice_…` id.
4. Synthesise 20 lines per character with `modelId: inworld-tts-2`, `voiceId`, text = `[say in a <delivery note> way] <line>`, `deliveryMode: BALANCED`; 5 concurrent. 60 lines ≈ 3,600 chars ≈ 0.09 USD at list, covered by the free minutes.
5. Blind A/B against the Gemini WAVs on two questions: same character across the 20 lines; delivery note audible. Also check the 48 kHz output's native rate.

Self-host VoxCPM2 on Modal (expected ≤0.5 USD) **[est]**:
1. Modal function with an L4 or A10G (24 GB), image with `voxcpm` from https://github.com/OpenBMB/VoxCPM , weights `openbmb/VoxCPM2` (~8 GB VRAM) baked into the image; `min_containers=0`.
2. Design: for each character generate 3 candidates of `"(<description>)<preview line>"`, keep the best WAV as `reference_wav_path`.
3. Lines: `"(<delivery note>)<line>"` with the reference WAV (controllable cloning); 60 lines at RTF ~0.3 is ~1–2 GPU-minutes plus a cold start; ≈ 0.05–0.10 USD, within Modal's monthly free credit.
4. Same blind A/B; additionally measure drift: speaker-embedding cosine between line 1 and line 20 per character (any ECAPA/WavLM embedder), reported alongside the Gemini and Inworld values.

Optional third at ~0.05 USD: Fish `voice-design-1` for 3 characters (0.03 USD) + 60 lines on `s2.1-pro-free` (0 USD), persisting via `POST /model`; tells us whether the design→clone chain works before relying on it.
