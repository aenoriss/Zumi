# Text-to-audio for Zumi panel ambience and music loops — state of the market, 2026-10-15

Research date: 2026-10-15. Primary sources only (model cards, licence files, GitHub READMEs, provider docs and pricing pages, Artificial Analysis leaderboard pages). Where a page could not be read (client-rendered, gated, 404) that is flagged and no number is given. Requirements: per panel one 10–30 s ambience or music loop from a mood/scene prompt, seamlessly loopable or crossfadable, commercial licence, API or self-hostable on Modal; stereo or ambisonic output is a plus for Meta Quest 3.

## Executive summary

1. Meta has **no commercially usable text-to-audio or text-to-music model** on 2026-10-15. Meta Model API lists Muse Wonder/Spark/Image/Voice Transcribe/SAM 3.1/Glimmer, none with audio output. AudioCraft (MusicGen, AudioGen, MAGNeT, JASCO, MusicGen-Style) weights are all **CC-BY-NC 4.0**; Movie Gen Audio (13B, 45 s, SFX+ambience+music) was never released; Muse Video has "native audio" only inside video, in preview, no API.
2. Only one API has a native seamless-loop switch: **ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2** (`loop: true`, 0.5–30 s, 40 credits/s = $0.12/min, royalty-free, on fal at $0.002/s). It covers exactly the 10–30 s ambience brief ("rainy alley, distant sirens, synth drone"). Cost for a 30-panel comic (30 × 20 s): **$1.20**.
3. For musical loops the cheapest strong closed options are **Google Lyria 3 Clip** ($0.04 per fixed 30 s stereo clip, AA Elo 1080 for Lyria 3 Pro / 1078 for 3.5; no loop parameter, SynthID watermark) and **ElevenLabs Music v2.5** ($0.15/min, 3 s–10 min, `force_instrumental`, Elo 1001–1021; self-serve plans exclude "Studio Games", film, TV). Stable Audio 2.5 is $0.20/clip on fal (Elo 984); Stable Audio 3 Large (Elo 1014) is API/fal only with no published price. Suno (Elo 1148, #1) and Udio have **no developer API**; MiniMax's music API closed to new users 2026-08-20.
4. Open weights with a clean commercial licence: **ACE-Step 1.5** (MIT, 10 s–10 min, <4 GB VRAM, "<2 s per full song on A100"), **Stable Audio 3 Small SFX / Small / Medium** (Stability Community License, free under $1M revenue, 0.6B/2B, "<2 s on H200", inpainting + continuation = seam repair for loops), **HeartMuLa-oss-3B** (Apache 2.0, 48 kHz stereo, RTF ≈ 1.0), **MiniMax Music 3** (Community License, free under $20M revenue but must display "MiniMax-Music3" in the UI). Not usable: YuE2 (CC-BY-NC, companies need a licence), TangoFlux (non-commercial), AudioLDM 2 (CC-BY-NC-SA), all Meta models.
5. Leaderboard (Artificial Analysis AA-Music-Instrumental v1.1, read 2026-10-15, blind private evaluators): Suno v6 1148, Suno v6-mini 1114, Mureka V9.5 1084, Lyria 3 Pro 1080, Lyria 3.5 1078, Eleven Music 1021, Stable Audio 3 Large 1014, MiniMax Music 3.0 1000 (anchor), Stable Audio 3 Medium 996, Stable Audio 2.5 984, Stable Audio 3 Small 946, Lyria 2 926, MusicGen 868. No arena exists for sound effects/ambience; AudioCaps FAD tables are stale (no 2026 entries).
6. Spatial: no text-to-FOA model with released weights was found (ImmerseDiffusion/Apple, Diff-SAGe, SonicMotion are papers only). Practical path: generate stereo (every option above is stereo except AudioGen/AudioLDM 2), spatialise at playback with the Quest audio SDK.
7. Recommendation: Meta option — none. **Ambience: ElevenLabs SFX v2 with `loop: true`** ($1.20/comic). **Music loops: Lyria 3 Clip** ($1.20/comic, crop 30 s → 20 s and crossfade) with ElevenLabs Music v2.5 ($1.50/comic) as the instrumental-controlled alternative. **Open fallback on Modal L40S ($1.95/h): Stable Audio 3 Small SFX + ACE-Step 1.5**, estimated ≤ $0.35/comic including cold start (estimate from published speeds, not a measurement).
8. Uncertainties: no vendor documents how its loop or crossfade sounds; Stable Audio platform pricing page is client-rendered (fal price used); Lyria RealTime is experimental and unpriced; ElevenLabs Music "Studio Games" exclusion may or may not catch an interactive Quest comic — ask ElevenLabs; Stable Audio 3 SFX max duration is not stated on the gated card.

## E. Recommendation for Zumi

**Meta option: none.** State it as "Meta provides the headset and the image layer; audio is third-party".

**Ambience (most panels): ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2, `loop: true`, `duration_seconds: 20`, `prompt_influence ≈ 0.3–0.5`.** It is the only API with a documented seamless-loop switch at 10–30 s, 40 credits/s = **$1.20 per 30-panel comic**, royalty-free with no plan restriction stated; identical price on fal ($0.002/s) if you want one vendor account. Caveat: WAV 48 kHz is "for non-looping effects", so loops arrive as MP3 44.1 kHz; stereo is not documented — check one output's channel count before committing.

**Musical loops (tense drones, themes): Lyria 3 Clip** at **$0.04 per 30 s stereo clip ($1.20/comic)**, Elo ~1080 family, best quality per dollar on the AA board; prompt "Instrumental only, no vocals"; crop to 20 s with a 1–2 s equal-power crossfade. Alternative when you need explicit instrumental control and exact length: **ElevenLabs Music v2.5** (`force_instrumental`, `music_length_ms: 20000`, **$1.50/comic**; avoid fal for it, whole-minute rounding makes it $18). Check with ElevenLabs whether an interactive Quest comic counts as a "Studio Game" under the Music Terms; if it does, Lyria is the safer licence.

**Open fallback on Modal: Stable Audio 3 Small SFX (ambience) + ACE-Step 1.5 2B turbo (music)**, both stereo-capable, <6 GB class, Community License (<$1M revenue, outputs owned) and MIT respectively. Estimated **≤ $0.35 per comic on an L40S** including cold start (from published H100/H200/A100 speeds, not measured). Make loops by generating 24 s and either (a) crossfading the last 4 s into the first 4 s, or (b) with Stable Audio 3, inpainting the seam using its multi-segment inpainting. HeartMuLa (Apache 2.0, 48 kHz stereo) is the quality candidate if RTF improves; MiniMax Music 3 only if the "MiniMax-Music3" UI attribution is acceptable.

**Quest 3 spatial:** no released text-to-ambisonics model (ImmerseDiffusion, Diff-SAGe, SonicMotion: papers, no weights stated). Ship stereo loops and spatialise at runtime (ambient bed as head-locked stereo, point sources as mono stems from a second SFX call). https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/immerse-diffusion-generative

**Cost per 30-panel comic (one 20 s loop each):** ElevenLabs SFX $1.20 · Lyria 3 Clip $1.20 · ElevenLabs Music $1.50 · Lyria 3.5 $2.40 · Stable Audio 2.5 (fal) $6.00 · Soundraw $9.00 · Mubert Trial $14.70 · ACE-Step on fal $0.12 · Modal self-host ≈ $0.04–0.36 (estimate).

**Uncertainty.** Loop quality is undocumented everywhere; run a 10-prompt A/B before choosing. Stability's own API price for Stable Audio 2.5/3 Large was unreadable (client-rendered), so the fal $0.20 stands in. Lyria RealTime (48 kHz stereo endless stream) would fit an "infinite ambience" design but is experimental and unpriced. The AA board measures music preference, not ambience; MusicGen's last place is the only evidence that the AudioCraft family lags, and it is non-commercial anyway. Tencent SongGeneration and Qwen-Audio-3.0-Gen could not be verified (gated / no weights).
